Our Story

ZAAMM_GIFTS was founded by five close friends — Zoya, Ayman, Ayesha, Maryam, and Mariya — united by creativity, friendship, and a shared love for gifting. Together, we turned our passion into purpose, creating a space where handmade gifts could bring people closer and make every occasion truly special.

What began as a small idea between friends quickly grew into a full-fledged brand — built with trust, teamwork, and countless late-night brainstorming sessions. Each product we offer is carefully crafted, packed with love, and designed to carry real meaning for the person receiving it.

At ZAAMM_GIFTS, we don’t just sell gifts — we help you share feelings, stories, and moments in the most heartfelt way possible. From birthdays and anniversaries to just-because surprises, we’ve got something handmade for everyone.

This is just the start of our journey — and we’re so excited to have you be part of it.
